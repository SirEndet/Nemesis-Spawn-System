Manual · The Hunter Boss

The Hunter Boss

The Hunter Boss is a rare, stronger hunter with a cloaked approach, named escorts and a fight to the death.

Encounter essentials
  • Your shot breaks his cloaked approach and keeps him visible for the hunt.
  • Listen for the heartbeat and watch for nearby screen distortion.
  • His named escort can keep hunting after he dies.

Identity

Hunter faction Default name
Monolith Bogdan Kravets
UNISG Lyutomyr Kat
Military Taras Hrytsenko

Names are editable in Boss settings. See Faction variants for who hunts your faction.

Arrival

The initial default boss chance is 4% per hunt. Every four hunts without him adds two percentage points, and the 30th hunt without him guarantees him. A zero boss chance disables him.

By default he is unavailable until seven in-game days after the grace period. Normal boss scheduling also limits repeat appearances and does not allow another boss already alive in the world.

His default escort is three named hunters, separate from normal squad-size weights. Escort size zero makes him arrive alone. The boss and escort teleport in with arrival effects.

The approach

If you stand still, walk or crouch, he can circle toward your back while cloaked. Once behind you, he closes in rather than circling indefinitely. If you run, he can rush directly toward you instead.

Within about 20 m, his warning and attack delay depend on that approach: the behind-you sneak has a longer warning, while the rushing attack uses about 1.5 seconds. Reaching within 10 m also triggers the close approach's short warning. A shot from you breaks the approach and keeps him visible for the rest of the hunt; simply seeing him does not.

After his approach ends, the combat AI chooses threats normally. He can answer nearby NPCs shooting him rather than being permanently locked to you.

Fighting and rewards

His normal damage reduction is 30%, alongside applicable armour and escalation rules. He does 200% damage to other NPCs and mutants by default. His first two hits on the player are guaranteed misses under the shared boss setting.

He does not retreat because of low health. A player-credited kill leaves his body and can award a rare stash. His escort continues fighting after he dies, so finish or escape the encounter. Beating the boss hunt halves escalation.

Reload taunt

Reload while the boss is alive and within 50 m, and he can answer with a PDA taunt. This happens at most once per hunt; it is an encounter message, not a separate reload-damage effect.

Listen for his heartbeat and watch for presence distortion. See Loot and rewards for credit and Cloaking for visibility rules.
