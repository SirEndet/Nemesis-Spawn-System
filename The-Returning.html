Manual · The Returning

The Returning

The Returning lets named hunters survive in the story of your save: a hunter you drive away can return by name, at a higher title and rank, remembering where you met.

How a return is earned

A fresh named hunter starts at the lowest faction title and the game's Experienced rank. When your credited damage makes him break off, his identity and meeting map can be kept for a later hunt.

The default Return chance is 35% for an eligible hunt's leader. Returning is not guaranteed on the very next hunt. An ordinary NPC causing the retreat does not automatically earn you a return.

Rank progression

Stage Game rank
Fresh hunter Experienced
Later return Professional
Later return Veteran
Later return Expert
Later return Master
Top stage Legend

A hunter entering the top stage leaves ordinary hunts and waits for a separate Legend encounter. He no longer appears as just another ordinary named hunter.

AlifeTactics, when installed and selected, provides rank-based skill changes. Otherwise Nemesis's rank sliders add accuracy, vision and burst effects. This progression does not replace escalation or directly invent extra armour per rank.

Permanent retirement

A tracked hunter who dies has his name retired. A retreat keeps him in the story; a death ends that identity. Ordinary damage protection means named hunters normally retreat, while Legends can be killed in their boss encounter.

Turning The Returning off uses random named-hunter title selection instead of Returning progression. Its controls are on The Returning settings, and are included in difficulty presets.
