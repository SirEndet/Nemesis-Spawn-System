Hunts

A hunt is a recurring squad encounter that follows you until it is beaten or you escape.

The normal sequence

Stage Medium/default behaviour
Grace Eight in-game days before the first hunt of a new game.
Cooldown 24 in-game hours after an encounter; pressure can shorten the effective wait.
Early warning Signs normally give one in-game hour of notice.
Arrival warning Radio static, plus an optional PDA warning, shortly before spawning.
Arrival A squad normally appears 80–140 m away, at a spot with nobody within 60 m if there is one, else the one with the fewest NPCs.
Encounter Hunters chase, fight, flank, retreat and can follow across maps.
Time limit Six in-game hours by default.

The normal arrival warning precedes spawning by a random 30–120 seconds. Debug mode changes normal timing; it is not the default experience.

Where hunts can start

A normal hunt is held back while you are asleep, in dialogue, underground, or near a friendly/neutral base or trader camp. The base check uses its current relationships: an enemy base is not a refuge.

Once the arrival warning has been issued, entering a friendly base does not cancel it. Dialogue or sleep can delay arrival. A running hunt can enter a base after you, although other mods' safe-zone rules can still affect NPC combat there.

Spawn placement avoids bases themselves. Squad composition is described in Hunters and squads.

Ending a hunt

An ordinary hunt is beaten when its members are dead or gone. Surviving regular squadmates continue after the named hunters retreat. Running out the time limit counts as an escape. Exceeding the configured map-follow limit also counts as an escape.

The outcome changes escalation. A boss hunt remains a boss hunt even if its boss dies before the escort is cleared.

Only one hunt runs at a time, including a squad waiting to follow. Boss encounters use additional arrival rules: Hunter Boss, Emission Boss, Thunder Boss, Legends.
