Reserve your kills

Reserve your kills is enabled by default under Combat settings. It stops NPC looters from taking bodies earned through Nemesis's player kill/assist credit.

Without AlifePlus

A reserved body stays protected for up to two in-game hours, or until you have been more than 150 m away for three minutes. This is a reservation of the corpse for you, not a promise that no other game system can ever affect it.

With AlifePlus

AlifePlus's own Reserve your kills option, range and time rules govern. If its option is off, Nemesis does not impose a competing reservation through that integration.

This feature does not make uncredited NPC kills into player loot. See Loot and rewards for which bodies stay.
