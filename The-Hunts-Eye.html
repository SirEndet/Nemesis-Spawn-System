The Hunt's Eye

The Hunt's Eye combines three systems that make the next hunt more predictable, but can also bring it sooner.

System What it tracks
Signs before the hunt An early PDA warning from the faction coming for you.
The Mark Pressure attached to your actions against Nemesis hunters.
Targeted Ground Pressure attached to a particular map.

Shared limits

The Mark and Targeted Ground can shorten Time between hunts, but together cannot reduce it by more than 50%. They do not directly increase squad size, damage reduction or escalation.

For example, a configured 24-hour cooldown cannot be reduced below 12 hours by these two pressure effects alone. Other arrival conditions still apply.

Switching it off

The Hunt's Eye has its own enable switch. Turning it off disables its effects while retaining its meters for later use. It is not a command to erase your pressure history.

All three systems are explained on their own pages. Their controls appear together on The Hunt's Eye settings.
