Manual · Combat settings

Combat settings

These controls affect protection, accuracy, retreat and optional retaliation features. Read Escalation and protection before combining percentage settings. See Reserve your kills, Retreats and Thunder casting for their exceptions and interactions.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
Retreat health (%) 25 Health at which a named hunter breaks off and teleports away. The bosses never do.
Accuracy boost (%) 20 How much better they shoot than normal stalkers. Squadmates get half, the boss 15 more. 0 = off.
Regular stalker damage reduction (%) 0 How much less damage the regular squadmates take. 0 = normal stalkers.
Named hunter damage reduction (%) 25 How much less damage named hunters take, on top of their armour.
Escalation per cycle (%) 2 How much tougher each beaten hunt makes them. 0 = no growth.
Max escalation (%) 20 The most the hunts can ever add.
Max damage reduction (%) 70 Caps ordinary combined damage reduction. The Thunder Boss’s casting reduction replaces the ordinary reductions and this cap while active.
Halve escalation when a hunter kills you On Dying to a hunter halves the escalation cycle, never below 1. This consequence can survive reloading an older save; it does not halve every combat statistic.
Hunter armour class bonus 0.05 Adds the configured armour-class bonus; this differs from percentage damage reduction. See protection rules for member types and supported ballistics handling.
Hunter BR mitigation bonus (%) 10 Adds the configured bullet-resistance mitigation bonus. See protection rules for how armour and reductions interact.
Reserve your kills On NPCs leave your Nemesis kills alone. With AlifePlus, its own "reserve own kills" option decides.
Latest exe AI features On Hunters pick their target more cleverly and turn on whoever shoots them. Needs the latest modded exes.
Hunter attack pull 600 How readily named hunters turn on whoever shoots them. 0 = the game decides.
Boss attack pull 300 The same for the boss (kept low: he prefers you).
