Manual · Ghosts

Ghosts

Ghosts belong to the Emission Boss encounter. They hold you in an emission shelter while the encounter develops.

The ring and exits

The haunting places a ring of 12 ghosts around your shelter, normally at roughly 55 m. Large buildings can move that ring farther out, about 25 m beyond the farthest wall. Additional groups of ghosts guard detected doors, up to six exits.

The door search tries to distinguish low door openings from windows. Complex geometry can still make the encounter depend on the actual shelter shape.

Pushes and psy damage

Getting too close to the ring or an exit ghost pushes you back. Door ghosts normally react within 3.5 m. Each eligible push costs 4% of your full psy bar by default, regardless of ordinary protection, at most once every 1.5 seconds.

In a very small shelter, door ghosts do not push; the outer ring still applies. Standing outdoors inside the ring does not make the emission safe.

The ghosts are not an alternative route out of the emission. Stay in the actual shelter and prepare for the horde and boss.

Ending the haunting

The ghosts persist until the encounter ends or you change maps. They do not stop the boss from entering, and following him onto another map does not create a new haunting there.

Settings: Ghost psy damage. Related: Sieges, which use a different indoor check.
