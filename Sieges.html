Manual · Sieges

Sieges

Hiding in a building changes how a hunt approaches. Hunters can wait outside rather than immediately entering after you.

Waiting and storming in

Situation Default wait
Ordinary hunt 90 seconds indoors.
Hunter Boss escort 20 seconds indoors.
You shoot a hunter from inside The wait ends immediately.

Hunters spread to positions outside and can fight other threats while waiting. After the wait, they push in.

Briefly crossing a doorway does not repeatedly buy a fresh siege timer: the ordinary reset requires about 10 seconds outside. A small awning is not a reliable way to reset the encounter.

Bosses and shelters

The Hunter Boss does not wait with his escort; his indoor approach heads toward you. The Emission Boss's ghosts require a real emission-safe shelter, which is a different check from ordinary indoor siege behaviour.

A roof alone does not guarantee that every shelter-related feature treats a place as a building. Other map geometry and shelter rules still matter.
