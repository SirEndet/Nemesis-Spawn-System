Loot and rewards

When a body stays

Regular squadmates and bosses can leave lootable bodies when the player receives kill credit. Named hunters normally retreat, and their bodies do not become ordinary rewards if something defeats their protection.

The short assist rule also credits a kill by another NPC when it happens within two seconds of your hit. A member killed without your credit normally teleports away.

A body that dies while still cloaked can disappear instead of leaving an invisible corpse. The normal boss becomes persistently visible after your shot, helping preserve the body you earn.

When an uncredited body teleports away, its dropped held weapon can disappear with it if still on the ground. A weapon already picked up by someone is left alone.

Rare boss stash

With Boss stash reward enabled, a credited boss kill can mark a rare yellow stash. The marked reward uses a real, openable stash box and the game's stash contents rather than a fixed guaranteed item list.

If the current map has no available suitable box, the reward can remain owed and be tried on a later map. The manual does not promise a particular gun, artifact or amount of money.

Shared rewards apply to eligible boss encounters, including Legends. Killing a boss does not automatically clear his living escort.

Protecting earned loot

Reserve your kills can keep NPC looters off your credited Nemesis bodies. AlifePlus's own corresponding option takes priority when that integration is present.
