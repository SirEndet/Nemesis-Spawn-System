Boss settings

The normal Hunter Boss uses these identity, chance and escort controls. Some shared rules, including guaranteed misses and stash rewards, also apply to other bosses. Escort zero allows a solo normal boss; chance zero disables natural normal-boss selection. See Loot and rewards.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
Boss name Bogdan Kravets The Monolith boss's name.
UNISG boss name Lyutomyr Kat The UNISG boss's name (when you play Zombified or Sin, or Monolith with UNISG picked).
Military boss name Taras Hrytsenko The Military boss's name (when the Military hunt you as a Monolith player).
Boss chance (%) 4 Chance that a hunt brings the boss. It grows the longer he stays away, and the 30th hunt without him brings him for sure. 0 = no boss.
Boss days after grace 7 Days after the grace period before a boss can appear.
Boss escort size 3 Named hunters who come with the boss. 0 = he comes alone.
Boss presence distortion (%) 40 A gentle screen wave when he is near (from 60 m). 0 = off.
Boss damage reduction (%) 30 How much less damage the boss takes.
Boss damage against NPCs (%) 200 How hard the boss hits other stalkers and mutants.
Boss guaranteed misses 2 Shared boss setting for the initial guaranteed misses against the player. See Boss encounters for its use by each encounter. 0 = none.
Boss stash reward On A player-credited boss kill can mark a rare yellow stash. Kill/assist credit and rewards deferred when no suitable box is available are explained in Loot and rewards.
