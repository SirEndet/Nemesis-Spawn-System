Manual · The Hunt's Eye settings

The Hunt's Eye settings

Controls for Signs, The Mark and Targeted Ground. Mark sleep/medicine effects remove a share of the current Mark; targeting drops remove meter points. The combined hunt-interval reduction is capped at 50%.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
The Hunt's Eye On Signs before the hunt, the Mark and Targeted Ground. Off: none of them.
Signs lead (hours) 1 In-game hours between the chatter on your PDA and the hunt. 0 = no signs.
Mark per boss kill 20 How much the Mark grows when you kill a boss, the emission boss or a Legend (of 100).
Mark per named hunter kill 10 The same for a named hunter you kill.
Mark per squad member kill 2 The same for a regular hunt squad member. Other stalkers add nothing.
Mark per forced retreat 8 How much the Mark grows when your hits make a named hunter break off.
Mark: hunts sooner (%) 30 How much sooner the next hunt comes at a full Mark.
Mark fades over (hours) 48 In-game hours for a full Mark to fade by itself. 0 = never.
Sleep eases the Mark (%) 50 How much of the Mark a sleep takes away.
Anti-psy eases the Mark (%) 35 How much of the Mark an anti-psy drug takes away.
Targeting per hunt 15 How much a map's targeting grows with every hunt on it (of 100).
Targeting per forced retreat 10 How much it grows when a named hunter gets away from you there.
Targeting per stay 10 How much it grows every Targeting stay hours you stay on the map.
Targeting stay (hours) 12 In-game hours for that step.
Cooling rate (x) 3 How much faster a map cools while you are away than it grows while you stay.
Targeting drop per map travel 20 How much every map's targeting drops when you change maps.
Targeting drop per anti-psy 30 How much this map's targeting drops with an anti-psy drug.
Targeting: hunts sooner (%) 25 How much sooner hunts come on a fully targeted map. With the Mark, never more than half.
Targeting: they know your ground (%) 100 How much further away they know your old spots on a fully targeted map.
