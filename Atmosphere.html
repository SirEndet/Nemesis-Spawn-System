Sound and screen effects

Nemesis uses sound, PDA chatter and screen effects to announce pressure and help you read encounters.

Effect Where to read more
Radio warning Hunts
Intercepted chatter Signs before the hunt
Directional hunter sound Psy hum
Nearby boss cue Boss heartbeat
Messages and optional device support PDA messages
Lightning and casting cues The Thunder Boss

Arrival effects

A brief silence precedes the radio warning when enabled. Its default length is one second. Monolith hunts have a call from their arrival position, and a psy screen swell can grow with escalation.

Default arrival effect strength is 40%, with a 10% per-cycle increase and a 50% maximum increase. These are presentation settings; they do not add hidden combat damage.

Boss presence

The normal boss's presence distortion starts within about 60 m, becoming strongest near 5 m. Its default strength is 40%, and it can be felt while he is cloaked.

The brief boss strike jolt has been removed and is no longer an available setting.

Volume controls

General settings separately controls warning, teleport, hum, lightning, heartbeat and arrival-call volumes. A volume of zero mutes the corresponding sound. Lightning volume covers both the Emission Boss's arrival strike and Thunder Boss effects.

Wearable Devices can flicker or blank screens during an arrival when that integration is enabled. These effects are optional.
