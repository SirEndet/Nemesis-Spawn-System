Chasing and combat

Hunters alternate between pursuing you and fighting through the game's combat AI. They sprint from a distance and approach with their weapon ready when closer.

Finding you

Hunt your last known position, on by default, lets hunters search the place where you were last seen, heard or involved in a hit. Turn it off and their pursuit heads directly toward your current position.

Remembered ground can reveal your position near an old fighting spot. Targeted Ground can widen that remembered area.

Handing over to combat

The default Fight range is 100 m. Seeing you and selecting you as an enemy allows the hunter to hand over to combat. A hunter who has seen you before his combat AI fully reacts can stop and face you rather than simply sprint into you.

Hunters can also fight hostile stalkers or mutants. Ordinary hunters eventually leave a distracting NPC fight and resume the chase unless the threat keeps engaging them. The normal boss has his own approach before ordinary combat takes over.

With Squad tactics enabled, extras can approach from flanking sides. A chasing hunter does not stop to loot bodies or help wounded NPCs.

Optional AI integration

AlifeTactics can supply its usual combat behaviour and rank effects. Latest exe AI features enables supported targeting and retaliation behaviour; the Hunter and Boss attack-pull settings influence how readily they turn toward a shooter.

These settings do not mean Nemesis replaces all combat AI. Bosses add encounter-specific actions such as the Thunder Boss's cast.
