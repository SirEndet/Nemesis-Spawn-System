Getting started

Requirements

  • S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly 1.5.3 with MCM, or GAMMA.
  • Current modded exes by themrdemonized, with their matching game scripts active.
  • xlibs 1.9.0 or newer, enabled in your mod manager.

The required callback features were added on 15 September 2026. Use a current engine release rather than treating that date as the recommended version. An updated executable alone is insufficient if conflicting files override its scripts. See Compatibility.

Your first hunt

With Medium defaults, a new game has eight in-game days of grace. A normal hunt then has a 24-hour cooldown after the previous hunt. The Hunt's Eye can shorten that wait later.

An intercepted Sign gives an early warning. Closer to arrival, radio static announces the hunt. A squad normally appears 80–140 metres away, out of sight and away from other NPCs where it can, and heads for you.

A normal hunt does not begin while you are underground, asleep, in dialogue, or near a friendly or neutral base or trader camp. This protection does not cancel a hunt once its arrival warning has already been issued.

The rules worth learning

  1. Named hunters usually retreat instead of dying to ordinary damage. Regular squadmates and bosses are killable.
  2. Clearing an ordinary hunt makes the next one stronger. Surviving its time limit makes the next one weaker. See Escalation.
  3. Moving to another map does not necessarily escape the squad: hunters can follow.
  4. Buildings buy time, but sieges can turn into an assault.
  5. Bosses have their own rules. Learn the Hunter Boss, Emission Boss, Thunder Boss and Legends separately.

Adjusting the experience

Open MCM → Nemesis Spawn System. Difficulty presets apply immediately. Thunder Boss settings are independent of the presets. Debug mode and Debug logs are off by default.

For installation, updates and removal, follow Installation and updating and Uninstalling.
