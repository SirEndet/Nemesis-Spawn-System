Manual · The Mark

The Mark

The Mark is a 0–100 pressure meter attached to you. Your actions against Nemesis hunters raise it, making later hunts come sooner.

What raises it

Credited action Medium default gain
Kill a boss, including a Legend +20
Kill a named hunter +10
Force a named hunter to retreat +8
Kill a regular hunt-squad member +2

Other stalkers add nothing. The player's kill/assist attribution matters; a nearby NPC fighting a hunter is not automatically credited to you.

The Mark is based on what you do to the hunters, not simply on how much damage they deal to you.

Effect on hunt timing

At a full Mark, the default timing reduction is 30%, with a proportionally smaller effect at lower values. Combined with Targeted Ground, the total cooldown reduction is capped at 50%.

The Mark does not directly increase hunter armour, squad size or escalation.

Reducing it

Method Medium default
Time A full Mark fades over 48 in-game hours.
Sleep Removes 50% of the current Mark.
Anti-psy item Removes 35% of the current Mark.

An item with psy protection can count, including Psy-Block. At Mark 80, the default anti-psy reduction removes 28 and leaves 52; it does not subtract a fixed 35 points.

The Mark fades over value can be set to 0 to stop natural fading. PDA messages announce its onset, strong pressure and disappearance.
