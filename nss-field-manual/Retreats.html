Manual · Retreats

Retreats

Ordinary named hunters are designed to break off rather than die to normal hits. Their default retreat threshold is 25% health.

Breaking off

A sufficiently damaging hit triggers their protection and retreat. With Retreat to cover first enabled, the hunter tries to run out of sight before teleporting away. If suitable cover is unavailable, he can run away along a clear route instead. The run is limited to a few seconds before the teleport.

A retreating named hunter does not become an ordinary wounded stalker waiting to be finished. Regular squadmates remain killable.

What a retreat changes

A retreat caused by your credited damage can:

  • Keep the named hunter for The Returning.
  • Add 8 Mark by default through The Mark.
  • Increase Targeted Ground and record remembered ground.

An NPC-caused retreat is not automatically credited to the player for those systems.

A hunt can continue after its last named hunter leaves if regular squadmates are still alive. When the whole encounter ends, its outcome determines escalation.

Bosses

Bosses fight to the death rather than retreating because their health is low. A hunt's time limit can still make a boss leave. See Bosses and Loot and rewards.
