Difficulty and presets

Difficulty is selected at the top of General: Easy, Medium or Hard. Medium is the default for the current version, 1.0.3. Changing a controlled value yourself can make the selection show Custom.

Immediate application and backup

A preset applies as soon as you select it. It does not wait for Apply, and Cancel does not undo it.

The Automatic Backup is made only when you leave Custom for Easy, Medium or Hard, and it includes pending slider edits. Switching between Easy, Medium and Hard never overwrites it, so Load settings from Automatic Backup always restores your last Custom settings.

Presets affect the values listed below. Check boxes, volumes, presentation effects and Thunder Boss settings are left alone. Signs lead is the same across difficulties. Do not assume every number is a universal percentage adjustment; use the exact table.

Existing games

The current 1.0.3 defaults do not automatically overwrite existing saved values. Select Medium deliberately to apply its preset-controlled values. Review other pages separately for values outside the presets.
