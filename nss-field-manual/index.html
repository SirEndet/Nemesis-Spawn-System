Nemesis Spawn System — Field Manual

Welcome to the English Field Manual for Nemesis Spawn System (NSS) for S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly and GAMMA. The current version covered by this manual is 1.0.3.

Nemesis adds recurring hunters who track you across the Zone. Named hunters retreat and return stronger, bosses bring distinct encounters, and your actions affect how soon the next hunt begins.

Boss encounters

Encounter What makes it different
The Hunter Boss A cloaked approach, named escorts and a fight to the death.
The Emission Boss Shelter ghosts, an approaching horde and a boss arriving through an emission.
The Thunder Boss A storm-gated visitor with lightning volleys and health regeneration while casting.
Legends Returning hunters who reach the highest rank and come back alone as bosses.
