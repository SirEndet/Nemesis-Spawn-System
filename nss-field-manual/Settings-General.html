General settings

General contains difficulty selection, backup restore, arrival warnings, sound/effect controls and optional device integrations. Read Difficulty and presets for immediate application and backup behaviour. Related features: Atmosphere, Psy hum, Boss heartbeat, PDA messages.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
Difficulty Medium Easy, Medium or Hard; immediately applies the values listed in Difficulty and presets. Cancel does not undo the selection.
Load settings from Automatic Backup – Puts back your last Custom settings, saved when you left Custom for a difficulty (unapplied ones too). Switching between difficulties never overwrites them. The box unticks itself.
HD icon Off Sharper portrait on the PDA messages.
Arrival warning audio On Radio static shortly before a hunt.
Arrival warning PDA Off A PDA message from an unknown frequency shortly before a hunt.
Arrival warning volume 70 Loudness of the warning static.
Teleport sound volume 50 Loudness of the teleport sound. 0 mutes it.
Psy hum volume 10 Loudness of the psy hum the nearest named hunter carries.
Lightning volume 60 Every lightning in the mod: the strike when the emission boss arrives, and the Thunder Boss's thunder and bolts.
Heartbeat volume (%) 60 The heartbeat near the boss. 0 = off.
Heartbeat top rate (bpm) 140 How fast it beats when he is on you (120 to 180).
Arrival call volume (%) 50 The Monolith's call where a hunt lands. 0 = off.
Arrival screen effect (%) 40 The psy swell when a hunt lands. 0 = off.
Arrival escalation per cycle (%) 10 How much heavier the arrival gets each cycle.
Arrival maximum increase (%) 50 The cap on that.
Silence before the warning On The world goes quiet before the radio warning.
Silence length (s) 1 The dead air before the radio (0.5 to 1).
Wearable Devices mod Malfunction On With the Wearable Devices mod: your device screens flicker when a hunt spawns, and go black for 2 s when the boss comes.
Messages on the Wearable Devices PDA mod On With the Wearable Devices PDA mod (which hides the game's message popups): every Nemesis message also goes to its Messages page.
