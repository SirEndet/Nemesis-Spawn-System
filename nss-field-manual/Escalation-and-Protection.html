Escalation and protection

Escalation is the persistent hunt cycle. It is separate from Returning ranks, The Mark and Targeted Ground.

How the cycle changes

Outcome Cycle change
Ordinary hunt beaten +1, regardless of whether you or other NPCs finished its members.
Boss hunt beaten Halved, rounded down, never below 1.
Hunt escaped through its time limit −1, never below 1.
Hunt escaped by exceeding map-follow allowance −1, never below 1.
Killed by a hunter, with death halving enabled Halved, never below 1. The death consequence can survive reloading an older save.

Killing the boss while his escorts are still fighting does not immediately finish the whole hunt. The encounter remains a boss hunt when its final outcome is decided.

Escalation does not fade simply because time passes.

Damage protection

The ordinary protection calculation combines armour mitigation, the member type's damage reduction and escalation. Named hunters and bosses can receive 2% escalation protection per cycle after the first, capped at 20% by default. Regular squadmates do not receive this escalation protection.

Setting Medium default
Regular stalker damage reduction 0%
Named hunter damage reduction 25%
Hunter Boss damage reduction 30%
Emission Boss damage reduction 30%
Maximum ordinary damage reduction 70%
Hunter armour class bonus 0.05
Hunter BR mitigation bonus 10%

Armour class and percentage mitigation are different. In GAMMA, the relevant ballistics protection can depend on the player's equipment; named hunters and bosses copy protection where supported, while regulars keep their own armour. Do not add the listed percentages as if they were all one flat reduction.

Thunder is an exception: his 90% casting reduction replaces the ordinary reductions and their cap while casting. He also has a separate NPC damage reduction setting. See The Thunder Boss.
