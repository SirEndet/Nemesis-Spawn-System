Manual · Map following

Map following

A running hunt can follow you to another map. Walking through an exit, fast travel and guides can all count.

Default behaviour

  • Maps they follow you: 2.
  • Surviving members keep their remaining health.
  • The hunt time limit continues; travelling does not start a fresh hunt.
  • Hunters normally approach from the side you arrived from.

After arrival, you must move 40 m from the entry point before the follow countdown starts. The squad then arrives after 60–120 seconds, normally 70–140 m away.

Dialogue, sleep and underground maps hold followers back. Entering a friendly base does not cancel a running follow.

Escaping

With the default allowance, the squad can follow twice. A further map change shakes it off and counts as an escape, lowering escalation by one cycle. Set the follow allowance to 0 to make a map change an escape.

If every named hunter is already breaking off when you leave, there may be no following squad; the encounter can instead count as beaten.

Boss encounters

The Hunter Boss follows as part of his hunt. The Emission Boss can follow too, but does not create a new ghost ring on the destination map.
