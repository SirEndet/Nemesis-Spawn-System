Manual · Signs before the hunt

Signs before the hunt

Signs are intercepted chatter on your PDA before an ordinary hunt. They give you time to prepare and indicate which faction is coming.

Lead time

The default Signs lead is one in-game hour. The hunt waits until that lead time has elapsed. Set it to 0 to disable Signs.

Signs are part of The Hunt's Eye. Turning off that system disables them too.

Signs versus arrival warnings

Warning Timing and control
Sign Early intercepted chatter, controlled by Signs lead.
Arrival audio Radio static shortly before the squad arrives; enabled by default.
Arrival PDA warning An additional short arrival warning; disabled by default.

Turning off the optional arrival PDA warning does not by itself turn off Signs. Likewise, a Sign does not mean the squad is already standing nearby.

Debug mode still shows the Sign but bypasses the normal wait; see Debug & tools settings.
