Manual · Legends

Legends

A Legend is a returning hunter who has reached the highest rank. He keeps his own name and comes alone as a boss.

Encounter essentials
  • A Legend is a returning named hunter, arriving alone as a killable boss.
  • His death permanently retires that identity.
  • Credited kills can earn the shared boss stash reward.

Reaching Legend

The Returning advances a named hunter through the ranks as the player forces him to retreat. When he enters the Legend stage, he leaves ordinary hunts and waits for a separate arrival.

A Legend is not an extra member of a normal squad.

Natural appearance

Setting Medium default
Legend chance 2%
Legend roll every 2 in-game hours
No Legend after a boss 12 in-game hours

Waiting Legends are checked at the roll interval. A Legend cannot arrive during another hunt or its warning, while another boss is alive, or during the boss lockout. A roll interval of 0 prevents natural Legend visits.

The encounter

A Legend arrives alone, with boss rules: he fights to the death, uses the shared guaranteed-miss setting, and can award a stash for a credited kill. Beating the boss hunt halves escalation.

When a real Legend dies, that hunter's name is retired permanently. See Loot and rewards.

Manual encounters

Debug & tools can force a waiting Legend or trigger a roll. With no Legend waiting, those choices can create Testing Legend, a temporary stand-in who retires no real name. That stand-in is not part of normal name progression.
