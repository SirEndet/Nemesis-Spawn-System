The Emission Boss

Ilarion Hrech is an emission-linked boss who can surround your shelter with ghosts before arriving with a horde.

Encounter essentials
  • Stay in a real emission-safe shelter; the ghost ring does not protect you from the emission.
  • Expect the horde before the boss, with possible pressure from multiple exits.
  • The boss does not cloak, and changing maps does not create another ghost ring.

When he appears

When an emission meets a running or due hunt, the default 100% Emission boss chance can replace it with this encounter. A separate 6% extra chance per emission can bring him without a hunt, subject to the grace period and normal safety gates.

For most player factions he is Sin. For Monolith players he follows the Military/UNISG hunter selection; Zombified and Sin players receive UNISG. His display name is separately configurable.

He does not start a second encounter while another boss is active.

Encounter sequence

  1. The dangerous part of the emission begins.
  2. Get into a building that is a real emission-safe shelter and remain there briefly.
  3. Ghosts form a ring and guard exits.
  4. The horde approaches before the boss.
  5. He arrives in lightning as the event's effects fade, then comes for you.

If you never get indoors within about 60 seconds, he can arrive without the ghost setup. Staying outside still leaves you exposed to the emission itself.

His arrival follows the emission's own progression, so it should not be treated as a simple fixed stopwatch from the first warning. The ghost encounter lasts at least 20 seconds before his arrival; the horde is scheduled 20 seconds before him.

Fighting him

He walks on his approach, does not cloak or sneak, and knows where you are. The emission spares him without making all other members of his faction immune.

Setting Medium default
Boss damage reduction 30%
Damage reduction against NPCs/mutants 70%
Horde size 6
Horde flank share 30%
Ghost psy damage 4% of the full psy bar per eligible push

He and his horde do not fight each other. Shared boss guaranteed misses and stash rewards apply. He fights to the death, although the hunt can still end through its normal limits. Ghosts remain until his hunt ends or you change maps; if he follows, the destination does not get a new ring.

Reload taunt

Reload while he is alive within 50 m, and he can respond with his own PDA taunt. The shared boss rule allows this at most once per hunt.
