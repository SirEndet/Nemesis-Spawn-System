Bosses

Bosses are killable encounters that fight to the death rather than retreating from low health. Each type has its own arrival conditions and mechanics.

Boss Default identity Main mechanic
Hunter Boss Bogdan Kravets / Lyutomyr Kat / Taras Hrytsenko Cloaked approach and named escorts.
Emission Boss Ilarion Hrech Ghosts, a horde and an emission-timed arrival.
Thunder Boss Danylo Raskat Storm visits, lightning casting and healing while his hand is raised.
Legend A returning hunter's own name A solo boss encounter at the top Returning rank.

A hunt's time limit can still end a boss encounter. Only one boss is allowed at a time; individual boss types have further scheduling restrictions.

Boss settings include the normal boss chance, escort, reduction, guaranteed misses and stash reward. Emission, Thunder and Returning have their own pages.

For credited bodies and stash rewards, see Loot and rewards. Beating a boss hunt halves escalation, rather than applying the ordinary hunt's +1 cycle.
