Hunters and squads

Named hunters and regulars

Type Behaviour
Named hunter Has a title and name; protected from ordinary lethal damage and retreats when sufficiently hurt. Can progress through The Returning.
Regular squadmate A killable stalker. Can continue the hunt after named members leave. Does not gain the named hunters' escalation protection.
Boss A separate, killable encounter leader with boss-specific behaviour. See Bosses.

A normal squad has one to five stalkers. Its first member is named. Each extra has a 25% base chance to be named, with regional adjustments when enabled.

Squad-size weights

Squad size Default weight
1 50
2 40
3 25
4 20
5 10

These are relative weights, not percentages, and do not need to total 100. Boss escorts use their own size setting.

Area scaling

With Scale hunts by area enabled, low-danger areas cap normal squads: tier 0 areas such as Cordon and Swamps allow at most two members, and tier 1 areas such as Garbage allow at most three. Higher-danger areas increase the chance of named extras: +10 percentage points at tier 4, +20 at tier 5. Unknown maps use the neutral middle tier. Boss hunts are excluded from this scaling.

Titles and identity

Titles follow the hunter faction. With The Returning enabled, a fresh named hunter starts at the game's Experienced rank, then climbs through Professional, Veteran, Expert and Master before entering the Legend stage.

Titles describe the returning hunter's progression; they are distinct from escalation, which belongs to the hunt cycle. A regular stalker with a personal name is still not necessarily a named Nemesis hunter.
