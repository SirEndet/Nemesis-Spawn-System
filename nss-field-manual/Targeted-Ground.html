Manual · Targeted Ground

Targeted Ground

Targeted Ground is a 0–100 meter for each map. Fighting hunts in one area and staying there too long makes that map more heavily targeted.

Building pressure

Event Medium default
Hunt fought on the map +15
Named hunter forced to retreat there by your hits +10
Staying on the map +10 every 12 in-game hours

Effects

At full targeting, hunts come 25% sooner by default. With The Mark, the combined reduction to the hunt interval is capped at 50%.

Targeting also expands remembered ground. The default full-targeting bonus is 100%, doubling the remembered-spot radius from 40 m to 80 m. This is not a global vision increase for every NPC.

Targeted Ground does not directly make squads larger or armour stronger.

Cooling a map

Maps cool while you are elsewhere, at a default rate three times the staying-related growth rate. Each map change drops targeting on every map by 20 points. An anti-psy item drops targeting on your current map by 30 points.

These targeting drops are points on the map meter. They differ from the Mark's percentage-of-current-value reductions.

PDA messages announce when the current map reaches the targeted threshold of 50 and when it cools to 25.
