Manual · Remembered ground

Remembered ground

When your hits force a named hunter to retreat, Nemesis can remember where you were standing.

What is remembered

Up to five spots per map are retained, and each is forgotten after seven in-game days. A later hunt finding you near one can know where you are instead of searching your last known position. Extra hunters can take wider flanks, and a PDA line can warn you that they remember the place.

The normal remembered radius is 40 m.

Interaction with Targeted Ground

Targeted Ground can enlarge that radius. With the default 100% increase at full targeting, the radius can reach 80 m. This is awareness around remembered spots, not an increase to every sight range in the game.

Remembered ground and The Returning are related but different: one records a fighting location, while the other preserves the identity and progression of a hunter.
