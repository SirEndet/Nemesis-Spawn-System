Frequently asked questions

Why will this hunter not die?

Ordinary named hunters have protection intended to make them retreat. Regular squadmates and bosses are killable.

Does clearing a hunt make the next one harder?

An ordinary beaten hunt increases the cycle. A beaten boss hunt halves it. Timing out or escaping beyond the follow allowance lowers it by one. See Escalation.

Can I hide in a base?

A friendly/neutral base can prevent a normal hunt from starting. Entering one after the arrival warning does not cancel the hunt. See Hunts.

Can I escape by changing maps?

Hunters can follow twice by default. Going beyond the allowance escapes the hunt. A follow allowance of zero makes map travel an escape.

Why did Medium not automatically replace my old settings?

Existing saved values remain yours. Select Medium deliberately if you want that preset. It applies immediately; Cancel does not undo it.

Why did the Thunder Boss ignore my difficulty choice?

His settings are outside the difficulty presets. Use Thunder Boss settings.

Why did a returning hunter become a solo boss?

He reached Legend. Top-rank Returning hunters leave ordinary hunts and wait for that encounter.

Does the Mark grow because I took damage?

The Mark grows from credited kills and forced retreats against Nemesis members. See The Mark.

Does an anti-psy drug remove 35 Mark points?

It removes 35% of your current Mark by default. Targeted Ground uses its own point reductions.

Do I need both downloads?

No. The player and commented-source packages have the same gameplay. Install one. See Installation.

Can I uninstall during a hunt?

Use Prepare for uninstall, wait for confirmation, then save before removing the mod.
