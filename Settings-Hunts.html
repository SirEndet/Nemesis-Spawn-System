Manual · Hunts settings

Hunts settings

These controls set ordinary hunt timing, placement, composition and pursuit. See Hunts, Hunters and squads, Map following, Cloaking and Faction variants. Grace, cooldown and hunt duration use in-game time. Squad-size values are relative weights, not percentages. Boss escort size is separate.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
Grace period (days) 8 Days of peace before the first hunt of a new game.
Time between hunts (hours) 24 Rest time after a hunt before the next one.
Hunt time limit (hours) 6 Survive this long and the hunters give up.
Spawn distance, min (m) 80 Closest a hunt can spawn to you.
Spawn distance, max (m) 140 Farthest a hunt can spawn from you.
Squad of 1 to 5, weight 50 / 40 / 25 / 20 / 10 How often each squad size appears. They don't need to add up to 100.
Extra is a named hunter (%) 25 Base chance for each extra member after the named leader to be another named hunter. Area scaling can adjust this chance.
Scale hunts by area On Smaller squads in rookie areas, more named hunters in the far north.
Hunt your last known position On When he loses you, he searches where he last saw or heard you. Off: he always heads for where you are.
Squad tactics On Squadmates flank you.
Retreat to cover first On A hurt hunter runs for cover before teleporting away.
Maps they follow you 2 How many times a hunt follows you to another map. 0 = never (changing maps is an escape).
Hunter cloak (experimental) On Named hunters turn invisible while stalking you from far away.
Fight range (m) 100 When they see you within this distance, they stop chasing and fight.
Monolith players are hunted by Military Selects Military or UNISG hunters for Monolith players from the next hunt onward. The Emission Boss uses the same faction selection.
