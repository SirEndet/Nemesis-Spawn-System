What is new in 1.0.3

This page summarizes the current version, 1.0.3. For the complete public history, see the Changelog.

New encounters and progression

  • The Thunder Boss: rare thunderstorm visits, lightning travel, warning charges and volleys, casting protection and regeneration.
  • The Returning: named hunters remember forced retreats, return stronger and retain their identities.
  • Legends: top-rank hunters become solo boss encounters.
  • The Hunt's Eye: early Signs, the player Mark and map-specific Targeted Ground.
  • Military hunts for Monolith players, with UNISG available as an option.

Settings and presentation

Presets apply immediately. The Automatic Backup is made only when you leave Custom for a preset, including pending slider edits, and switching between presets never overwrites it. Hunts now spawn at an empty spot in the spawn range, or the one with the fewest NPCs nearby.

The directional psy hum, optional Wearable Devices PDA messages, and Nemesis's own log file improve feedback and reporting.

Medium defaults

Setting Current default (1.0.3)

Hunt time limit 6 in-game hours
Retreat health 25%
Accuracy boost 20%
Boss escort 3
Emission horde 6
Anti-psy Mark reduction 35% of current Mark
Thunder casting regeneration 4% of full health per second
Debug mode / Debug logs Both off

See Settings for the complete values. Existing saved settings are not automatically reset.

Fixes and refinements

Version 1.0.3 includes the boss-death crash repair and subsequent combat, lightning-cast, body/weapon cleanup and Interaction Dot Marks fixes. The old boss jolt was removed. Expected release-guard messages no longer look like critical failures.

These changes are described as player behaviour here; they are not a guarantee that no other mod or installation can cause a problem. See Troubleshooting.
