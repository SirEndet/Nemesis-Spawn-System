Manual · Compatibility

Compatibility

Required components

Component Requirement
Game Anomaly 1.5.3 with MCM, or GAMMA.
xlibs 1.9.0 or newer, enabled.
Modded exes Current themrdemonized release with matching scripts active.

The necessary callback features date from 15 September 2026. Their scripts are supplied in db/mods/00_modded_exes_gamedata.db0. Conflicting loose scripts can override them even if the executable itself is new enough.

Do not infer that a dependency is enabled from its presence on disk; check your mod manager. Exact mod-list names vary.

Optional integrations

Mod/system What Nemesis provides
AlifePlus Hunt-squad ownership compatibility and coordination with its kill-reservation option.
AlifeTactics Normal combat coexistence and an optional rank-effect source for Returning hunters.
GAMMA ballistics / Obrien GAMMA Overhaul Protection handling for Nemesis hunters alongside the player's ballistics chain.
GAMMA Psy rework Ghost psy damage through the rework's psy-health system.
Wearable Devices Optional arrival flicker and boss screen blanking.
Wearable Devices PDA mod Optional forwarding to its Messages page.
Interaction Dot Marks Removal of stale markers for Nemesis bodies/weapons that disappear.
G.A.M.M.A. No NPC Friendly Fire Hits it cancels between friendly or neutral factions are put back only for fights with the Thunder Boss; story NPCs, companions and traders are never affected.
GAMMA No Exos in the South Living hunt members are exempt while the hunt runs.

These integrations do not mean the optional mods are required. Nemesis does not redistribute Wearable Devices files or assets.

Appearance and AI differences

Cloaking requires the invisible stalker model to be available. If it is missing, do not expect the same invisible appearance.

Other mods' combat restrictions, safe zones and loot tables still matter. See Faction variants.

For missing dependencies, unexpected named-hunter deaths or missing messages, see Troubleshooting.
