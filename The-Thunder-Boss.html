Manual · The Thunder Boss

The Thunder Boss

Danylo Raskat is a rare storm visitor who arrives alone in lightning: a Loner against the world. Every faction fights him, Loners included, and he fights everyone near him, you too; killing him, or anyone he kills, costs no standing with any faction. He wears an Exo-SEVA Loner suit with electric sparks around his legs, and has no cloaked sneak approach. While his hand is raised, he regenerates 4% of his maximum health every second by default. Attack between casts to avoid fighting through both his healing and casting protection.

Encounter essentials
  • Keep moving: player-directed bolts aim at your recent position.
  • Attack between casts; a raised hand means healing and casting protection.
  • Healing restores maximum-health percentage points, while bolt damage removes a share of your current health.

Natural visits

Condition Default
Chance per eligible thunderstorm 15%
Minimum gap 5 in-game days
First encounter prerequisite At least one hunt has happened.
Competing encounters No other active hunt or boss.
Emission/psy-storm exclusion During the event and for 5 real minutes afterward.

His natural arrival is tied to a real thunderstorm. Rain alone is not a guarantee. Chance 0 stops natural visits; his enable switch controls the encounter.

Read the cast

His sparks intensify and a buzz gives about 1.5 seconds of warning before the raised-hand sequence. He puts away his weapon and raises a hand. The first volley follows about 1.5 seconds after the hand is raised.

A full cast has three volleys, normally three seconds apart, with two to five bolts per volley. After the cast ends, at least 20 seconds pass before the next cast can begin. A missing valid target can end a cast early rather than leaving him casting at nothing.

Player-directed bolts aim at where you were about half a second earlier. A bolt reaching within about one metre of its landing point can hurt you. Keep moving instead of trying to out-tank the whole sequence.

Health regeneration and casting protection

Thunder heals only while his hand is raised during a cast. The default rate is 4% of maximum health per real second, rather than 4% of his remaining health. The initial warning charge before his hand goes up does not give him this healing.

For example, if he starts at 40% health and spends five seconds with his hand raised, he can recover 20 percentage points and reach 60%, provided he takes no damage during that time. Healing cannot take him above full health.

While his hand is raised, his default 90% casting damage reduction replaces the ordinary reductions and their cap. The regeneration and casting protection end when the cast ends; he does not keep this regeneration during ordinary gun combat. This makes the gaps between casts the main opportunity to hurt him.

Regeneration while casting (%/s) controls the healing rate. Its range is 0–10% of maximum health per second; 0 disables this healing. The default is 4, and difficulty presets do not change it.

Targets and damage

Setting Default
Lightning range at the player 30 m
Lightning range at enemy NPCs/mutants 45 m
Player health lost per reaching bolt 7% of current health
Casting damage reduction 90%
Regeneration while hand is raised 4% of full health per second
NPC/mutant damage reduction 60% at all times

A wall between Thunder and the player can block a bolt's damage when the cover check detects the obstruction. Reaching the landing point alone is not enough to damage the player through that wall. Armour alone does not block the health drain.

Lightning bypasses ordinary armour protection. NPC volleys can kill their target on the final bolt. Story/protected NPCs, companions, traders and Nemesis hunters are excluded from his lightning targets.

The first volley can target you when he sees you within range. Later volleys favour suitable nearby NPC enemies; if none exists, they can target you again. This is not a guarantee of one NPC death on every volley.

His casting reduction replaces the ordinary reductions and their cap while active. Attack between casts, when he is not receiving that casting protection and regeneration.

Suppression after casting

When a cast ends, Thunder has a 50% chance to enter a 2.5-second suppression burst. His weapon bursts become longer where the supported engine feature is available, while his shot spread becomes much wider. It is intentionally inaccurate suppressive fire, not a guaranteed precision attack.

He can return to gun combat while his lightning cooldown runs. The next cast cannot begin until at least 20 seconds after the previous cast ended, and it still needs an eligible visible target. If the target is gone when his warning charge finishes, that charge can fade without becoming a cast.

Appearance, death and travel

His arrival uses five sky strikes at random moments within 0.7 seconds. An uncredited NPC kill uses the same lightning departure around his position instead of leaving a body. A player-credited kill leaves his body without that departure sequence. Loot credit includes the short player-assist window.

His settings are independent of difficulty presets. Lightning volume is on General settings; his combat controls are on Thunder Boss settings.
