Manual · Thunder Boss settings

Thunder Boss settings

Controls for Danylo Raskat, independent of difficulty presets. Lightning volume is on General. Casting reduction replaces ordinary reductions and their cap. Regeneration uses full health per second; bolt damage uses the player's current health per reaching bolt.

Options

Current defaults are for version 1.0.3. Existing saved values can differ.

Setting Default What it does
Thunder Boss On Enables his visits and lightning.
Thunder Boss name Danylo Raskat His displayed name.
Thunder Boss chance per storm (%) 15 One roll per eligible thunderstorm. 0 stops natural visits.
Days between his visits 5 Minimum in-game days between natural visits.
Thunder Boss damage reduction (%) 90 Damage reduction while casting; replaces other reductions and their cap.
Lightning damage (%) 7 Current health lost per bolt that reaches you, through armour.
Regeneration while casting (%/s) 4 Percentage of maximum health restored each real second while his hand is raised. Default 4; range 0–10. Set 0 to disable healing. It stops when the cast ends.
Lightning range (m) 30 Maximum distance for lightning aimed at you.
Lightning range at NPCs (m) 45 Maximum distance for lightning aimed at enemy stalkers or mutants.
Thunder Boss NPC damage reduction (%) 60 Damage reduction against NPCs and mutants at all times.

Understanding regeneration

The default value 4 restores 4% of maximum health per real second while Thunder has his hand raised. It is not a percentage of his remaining health and is not permanent healing between casts.

Five seconds of uninterrupted healing restores 20 percentage points, capped at full health. Incoming damage can reduce the net gain. Set the slider to 0 to stop this healing without turning off his lightning encounter. Read Health regeneration and casting protection for how it affects the fight.
